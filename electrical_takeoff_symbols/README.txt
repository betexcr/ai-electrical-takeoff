Electrical Takeoff Symbol Reference

27 symbol crops are included, renamed descriptively from their original screenshot timestamps.

Important handling notes:
- ceiling_fan_optional_pre_wire.png is a required detection class. Count the represented ceiling-fan/pre-wire location in the ceiling-fan symbol takeoff, and preserve its status as optional pre-wiring. The symbol alone is not proof that a physical fan is installed.
- wiring_run_electrical_REFERENCE_BOM.png represents wiring/connections, not an individual device. Keep it for cable-run analysis / optional bill of materials.
- fan_coil_unit_dedicated_outlet_REFERENCE.png includes an HVAC fan-coil callout and its dedicated outlet. Do not count the fan-coil unit itself as an electrical device; count the outlet where the plan shows it.
- Combined smoke/CO detector counts once under its combined type.
- The catalog lists the included symbol crops and handling guidance.

Not included: plumbing symbols (CW/HW/HB/SOV) and the attic access panel, because they are not electrical takeoff components.
